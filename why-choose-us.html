Why Choose Us

Safe, structured, and built for growth.

Henlopen Run Club helps middle school runners build confidence, endurance, and consistency in a supportive environment.

Sign Up View Calendar

Qualified Leadership

Led by student-athletes with real cross country experience and knowledge from strong coaching backgrounds.

Safe Environment

Sessions are planned around safe routes, organized structure, and responsible supervision.

Real Running Experience

Volunteer coaches have experience with training, racing, volunteering, and local running events.

What makes the club different?

  • Support for beginner, intermediate, and advanced runners
  • Structured sessions with warm-ups, drills, running, and cooldowns
  • Focus on confidence, fitness, and teamwork
  • End-of-summer 5K goal to give runners something exciting to work toward
