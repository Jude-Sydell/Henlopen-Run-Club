About the Developer

My name is Jude Sydell, and I am a sophomore at Cape Henlopen High School. I created this website as part of my goal to use technology, leadership, and community involvement to make a positive impact.

Through projects like Henlopen Run Club, I am learning how to take an idea and turn it into something real. I am especially interested in web development, startups, app development, and using code to solve real-world problems.

This project represents my interest in computer science, my passion for running, and my goal of building something meaningful for the community.
